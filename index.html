Scientific & engineering simulation

5–12 days

Model, runs, plots and a 4–8 page technical note — with every source file.

What you get

A zip with the input deck, mesh or model file, raw result tables, 6–12 publication-clean plots, and a short note covering objective, assumptions, boundary conditions, results and limits.

Circuit design

4–10 days

Schematic, BOM, SPICE sanity check and a pinout note. PCB layout as an add-on.

What you get

Schematic in KiCad / EasyEDA / Altium-export, annotated PDF, BOM with vendor links, SPICE check, and a one-page “how to test on the bench” note. Optional 2-layer PCB + Gerbers.

CAD design

3–8 days

Part or assembly, 2D drawing, STEP / STL and a revision pack.

What you get

Parametric model, exploded view for assemblies, a manufacturing drawing with defendable tolerances, and STEP + STL + native file. CAD tool named on the quote.

Print-ready PDF plus editable source. Paper figures follow journal column width. Brand kits include logo, colour tokens, two typefaces and three social templates.
